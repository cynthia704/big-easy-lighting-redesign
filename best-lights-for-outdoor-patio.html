LED Lighting Systems

Improve Your New Orleans, Louisiana Home’s Curb Appeal With Beautiful and Bright Lights

February 20, 20235 min readBy Big Easy Lighting

Are you looking for an outdoor lighting solution that can be customized to fit your patio’s decor? Do you want something reliable, with bright and long-lasting light bulbs?

Outdoor patios are an excellent addition to any home, providing a cozy spot for relaxation or entertainment. After all, who doesn’t like to have an outdoor retreat? But it wouldn’t be complete without appropriate lighting. The right lighting sets the mood for your outdoor space and provides additional safety for family and friends.

When choosing the best lights for your patio, there is a lot to consider in terms of style, brightness, durability, and energy efficiency. This guide from Big Easy Lighting will provide you with helpful tips so that you can choose the ideal lights that perfectly match your needs.

The Best Types of Lights for Outdoor Patios

The best type of lights for outdoor patios in New Orleans are weather-resistant fixtures that can stand up to humidity and heat. Lightings such as festive string lights, solar-powered lamps, lanterns, and post lights will bring a festive and attractive atmosphere to help enhance the ambiance while providing good lighting and visibility.

LED Lights Are Low-Energy and Long-Lasting

LED lights are an ideal lighting solution for outdoor patios in New Orleans because they are low-energy and long-lasting. LED bulbs are more efficient than traditional lightbulbs, and they last longer too, which makes them cost-effective over the long term.

Solar-Powered Lights Require No Maintenance and Illuminate the Area With a Soft Light

Solar-powered lights are an ideal option for outdoor patios in New Orleans as they require no maintenance, and provide a soft light, which is perfect for creating a cozy atmosphere.

Incandescent Lights Provide a Warm Ambient Glow, Ideal for a Romantic Setting

Incandescent lights are the best type of lighting for outdoor patios in New Orleans due to their warm ambient glow. They provide a romantic setting, perfect for special occasions or just days when you want to unwind with a glass of wine on your other patio.

Hanging String Lights Can Brighten Up the Patio While Adding a Whimsical Atmosphere

Hanging string lights are an excellent choice for outdoor patios in New Orleans. These lights can brighten up the space and provide a whimsical atmosphere that is perfect for both casual and formal events.

Waterproof Lights That Can Withstand Rain and Humidity During the Wet Season in New Orleans

When looking for the best type of lights for outdoor patios in New Orleans, it is important to consider the weather. The wet season in New Orleans can bring a lot of rain and humidity, so waterproof lights are definitely the way to go.

What Is the Purpose of Lights in Outdoor Patios?

Lights in outdoor patios are essential for creating a pleasant atmosphere and inviting guests into the patio. Outdoor lighting establishes a nighttime presence when entertaining friends, family, and strangers alike. The right type of light can create a mood that sets the theme of the evening while increasing safety.

Lights can also be used to show off landscaping elements or as task lighting for important amenities like chairs, grills, etc. Ultimately, lights are used to enhance one’s experience on their patio so they can enjoy it more and longer into the night.

How Much Do Outdoor Patio Lights Usually Cost in New Orleans?

Outdoor patio lights usually cost an average of $25-$50 in New Orleans. However, these prices can vary depending on the type and style of light you are looking for. LED lights tend to be a bit more expensive while Halogen bulbs often cost less.

You also have to consider how many lights you need and if you want patio lights that are designed specifically for outdoor use. Additionally, buying more than one set of patio lights can reduce the cost per light, making it an affordable option overall.

How Do I Choose Lights for My Outdoor Patio?

Choosing lights for your outdoor patio should depend on the size of the area, the type of atmosphere you want to create, and your budget. Consider different types of lighting such as hanging string lights for a romantic atmosphere, wall-mounted lanterns for a more classic look, or post lights for a modern touch.

Think about using solar-powered or low-voltage lighting fixtures to save energy and money. You can also shop around online to compare prices, and styles and read customer reviews. Once you’ve found an option that fits your needs and meets your budget, install it according to manufacturer instructions and with safety precautions in order to ensure many years of enjoyment in your outdoor space.

Frequently Asked Questions

What Are the Best Lights for an Outdoor Patio?

A layered mix works best: overhead string or downlights for ambiance, step and railing lights for safety, and accent lighting for features. We design the layers for your New Orleans patio.

How Do I Light a Patio Without Glare?

Use shielded fixtures, warm color temperatures, and dimmers so the light is comfortable. We plan placement to avoid glare while keeping the patio well lit.

Is String Lighting Good for Patios?

Yes. Commercial-grade string lighting gives a warm overhead glow that is hugely popular for New Orleans patios and courtyards, and we install it to last.

What Patio Lighting Is Most Durable?

Weather-rated LED fixtures and commercial-grade string lights last longest in our humid, storm-prone climate. We choose fixtures built for New Orleans conditions.

Can Patio Lighting Be Dimmed?

Yes. We install dimmable patio lighting and controls so you can set the right mood for dining, entertaining, or relaxing.
