Good questions

Before You Call

01How Much Does Landscape Lighting Cost in New Orleans?+

It depends on the size of the yard, the number of fixtures, and the design. A few accent lights cost far less than a full-property system. A free on-site estimate gives you an accurate number.

02What Is Low-Voltage Landscape Lighting?+

A transformer steps household power down to 12 volts, which makes the system safer, efficient, and easy to expand.

03Will It Hold Up to Storms and Humidity?+

Yes. We use corrosion-resistant, weather-rated fixtures with sealed connections built for the Gulf climate.

04Does Outdoor Lighting Use a Lot of Electricity?+

No. Low-voltage LED fixtures use very little power, so running costs stay low even with the whole yard lit.
