All about Kenner

About Kenner

Kenner is a Jefferson Parish city on the west side of Greater New Orleans, running from the Mississippi River in the south to Lake Pontchartrain in the north. It is the most populous city in the parish and home to Louis Armstrong New Orleans International Airport. Rivertown, its historic district near the river, and Laketown, its lakefront park, sit at opposite ends of the city.

Parish: Jefferson Parish

Landmarks: Rivertown Historic District, Laketown Lakefront Park, Heritage Park, Pontchartrain Center

Things to Do in Kenner

  • Coconut Beach Volleyball Complex: Sand volleyball complex in Laketown with leagues, tournaments, and charity events like the Manning Family Children's classic.
  • Pontchartrain Center: Kenner's convention and event center hosts home shows, cheer and sports championships, festivals, and concerts.
  • Kenner Planetarium and MegaDome Cinema: Saturday dome shows in Rivertown about stars, planets, and flying reptiles, plus seasonal laser shows.
  • Space Station Kenner and Rivertown Science Center: Walk through a life-size prototype of NASA's space station, then try hands-on weather and river exhibits.
  • Rivertown Theaters for the Performing Arts: Theatre 13 stages musicals and plays at this Minor Street venue, with a full 2026-27 season.

Notable Residents

  • Lloyd Price: 'Mr. Personality' R&B singer raised in Kenner, which celebrates an annual Lloyd Price Day.
  • Edmond J. Muniz: Founder of the Krewe of Endymion who later served as mayor of Kenner.
  • Chris Howard: Kenner-born running back who won a national title at Michigan and played for the Jaguars.
  • Arthur Maulet: NFL cornerback and Kenner native who starred at Bonnabel High School.

Fun Facts About Kenner

  • Minor Kenner founded the town in 1855 on land made up of three family plantations: Oakland, Belle Grove, and Pasture.
  • Rivertown is a sixteen-block historic district near the Mississippi River, with museums, theaters, and Heritage Park.
  • Louis Armstrong New Orleans International Airport sits inside Kenner's city limits, just south of Interstate 10.

Driving Directions

  • From Downtown New Orleans: Take I-10 West to Williams Boulevard (exit 223) in Kenner.
  • From Metairie: Take Veterans Memorial Boulevard west; it runs into Kenner and ends at Belleview Boulevard.
  • From Baton Rouge: Take I-10 East past LaPlace and across the Bonnet Carre Spillway bridge to Williams Boulevard (exit 223).

Public transit: Jefferson Transit (JeT) serves Kenner, including the E1 Veterans-Airport route and the E201 Kenner Loop, both of which stop at Louis Armstrong New Orleans International Airport.
